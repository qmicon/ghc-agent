Theme Design: Lipstick & Nail Polish Boutique

A sophisticated and elegant theme designed specifically for beauty and cosmetics stores, with a focus on showcasing lipstick and nail polish products. The design emphasizes luxury, femininity, and modern aesthetics.

Key Design Elements:

1. Color Scheme:
- Primary colors: Deep navy (#17313e) for headings and accents
- Secondary colors: Soft whites (#ffffff) for backgrounds
- Accent colors: Vibrant red (#db0022) for hover states and call-to-action
- Product overlays: Semi-transparent navy (#17313e) for product hover effects

2. Typography:
- Primary font: Montserrat (clean, modern sans-serif)
- Heading font: Playfair Display (elegant serif)
- Subtitle font: Dancing Script (feminine, decorative)
- Base font size: 16px
- Heading sizes: 26px-60px (scalable)

3. Layout Features:
- Full-width slideshow with overlay text
- Grid-based product display (2-5 products per row)
- Elegant product cards with hover effects
- Clean, spacious product detail pages
- Sticky header for easy navigation
- Newsletter popup for customer engagement

4. Product Presentation:
- Large, high-quality product images
- Color swatches for product variants
- Quick view functionality
- Wishlist and compare features
- Product countdown timers for promotions
- Sale and new product badges
- Product overlay effects on hover

5. Special Features:
- Instagram feed integration
- Social media sharing
- Multiple currency support
- Newsletter subscription
- Product quick view
- Wishlist functionality
- Product comparison
- Color swatch selection
- Countdown timers for deals

6. Mobile Optimization:
- Responsive design
- Mobile-friendly navigation
- Touch-friendly product browsing
- Optimized product images
- Easy-to-use mobile cart

7. Marketing Elements:
- Promotional banners
- Newsletter popup
- Social media integration
- Product countdown timers
- Sale badges and labels
- Instagram feed showcase
- Customer testimonials

8. User Experience:
- Clean, intuitive navigation
- Easy product filtering
- Quick add to cart
- Smooth checkout process
- Customer reviews integration
- Related products suggestions
- Recently viewed products

This theme is designed to create an immersive shopping experience that emphasizes the beauty and quality of cosmetic products while maintaining a professional and luxurious feel.
